        📄 Bulk Upload by Spreadsheet

        Apply many greetings at once from a spreadsheet.

        Your sheet needs EXT NUMBER and GREETING TYPE, plus an audio source for each row — either a GREETING NAME (a file in the Drive folder below) or TTS text (generated with AI). Fill only one per row; filling both is an error, and leaving both blank skips the slot. An optional VOICE column sets the AI voice for TTS rows.

        Download template
